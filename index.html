Privacy Policy for “Hacker's Quest”

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

Konya Kirsten
Email: hackersquest@icloud.com

2. In short

Hacker's Quest is an offline game. It does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

3. Data on your device

Your game progress is stored only on your device. I have no access to it. Apple may include it in your device backups, depending on your own settings.

4. Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

5. Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Contact and your rights

Because the game does not process any personal data about you, there is nothing I could provide, correct or delete on that basis. If you email me, I use your email address and your message only to reply, and I delete them afterwards (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

7. Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, I will update this page and the date above.
